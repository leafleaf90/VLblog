I needed a QR code the other day. A URL on a sticker. Nothing clever.

So I opened a “free” generator online. Preview, watermark, email field, create-an-account-to-unlock-HD. Same dance on the next site, different logo.

Generating a QR code is not hard. Finding a page that will just let you do it is.

I put a small generator on this site instead. If you want to build something similar into an app, I wrote about [QR generation in Vue](/2023/05/29/generate-qr/) a few years ago.

**[viktorlovgren.com/qr/](/qr/)**

Free. No account. No watermark. Runs in your browser, so the text never needs to leave your machine. Paste a URL or some text, download PNG or SVG, done.

That is the whole thing.
