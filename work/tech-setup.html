Small businesses and regular people get sold a stack they will never finish setting up. I help you pick a small set of tools that actually fit, then set them up.

That usually means a domain, email on that domain, a website if you need one, and AI or agents only where they save time.

What this can include

  • Domain registration and DNS that you can still explain a year later
  • Email on your own domain
  • A personal or small-business website
  • A restrained AI setup: which tools to pay for, which to skip, and a simple agent if there is a real repeatable task

What this is not

  • An operating system made of forty Notion templates
  • A custom app when a form would do
  • A retainer to do your digital transformation

How it works

  1. You describe what you are trying to do.
  2. I suggest a short stack and why.
  3. If you want, I set it up with you, or I do the parts that are annoying and hand you the keys.

I write about a lot of this on the blog: static sites, forms, hosting, and AI tools. The work here is the same idea, pointed at your situation. If you actually need a custom app, that is web app development.
