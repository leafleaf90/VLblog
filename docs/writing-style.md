# Writing style

How Viktor writes on this site. Use it for blog posts, Work pages, and other prose. The short Cursor rule is `.cursor/rules/writing-style.mdc`.

## How a piece usually starts

He walks in mid-problem, as himself:

- "I needed a QR code the other day. A URL on a sticker. Nothing clever."
- "I just wrapped up my own house build."

Not a persona brief: "You have a floor plan. You want to see the house before anyone starts knocking down walls."

## Tone

Dry, restrained, slightly crooked when it jokes. Specific and a bit awkward is better than balanced.

Fine: "My glorious second fail." / "or shall we call it _excitement?_"

Not fine: two images for one idea ("knocking down walls, or before the first brick goes down"). Same family as "when the hand turns into a decorative fork."

If humor is useful, make it brief. Prefer clarity.

## Sentences

Mix short lines with ordinary longer ones. Name objects: PDF, CAD export, watermark, email field, 25 cm from the curb.

Avoid:

- Studio talk: "photoreal pass", "the clean model is not enough." Say stills, AI render, a more realistic image.
- Voiceover process: "Send the drawing. I build the model. You walk through it, we revise…"
- Empty pivots: "So that is the job." Say what you mean: "I do the same thing for other floor plans."
- Vague this/that: "I was there for this one because it was mine." Say: "I was on site for the build. It was my house."
- Antithesis / not-X-but-Y: one sentence that sells by naming the rejected opposite. "The storefront is a system, not a pile of one-off pages." / "Custom where it matters. Stock where Shopify is already good." It reads like landing-page copy. Say the true thing. Drop the foil. If a limit matters, give it its own sentence with a reason, or put it in a list of things you actually do not do.

Process is numbered steps he actually runs, or one normal paragraph. Closers are a shrug: "That is the whole thing."

## Service pages

Still a person talking, not a landing page. First person is fine. "You" is for instructions, not for selling a feeling.

The contact form is a note. Do not imply file upload ("Send a floor plan" as a button). Ask, then email the drawing if it is a fit.

Do not invent case studies. If pictures are his house, say so.

## Pull quotes

Highlight an idea already in nearby body text. Factual quotes stay close to the body wording. Summary quotes may be tightened, but must not introduce new information only in the quote.

## When editing

Keep the author's meaning and tone. Do not increase joke density.
